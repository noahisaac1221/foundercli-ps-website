Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Overview

FounderClips ("we", "us", "our") operates a short-form video content platform that distributes curated clips via TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our use of the TikTok API and our content publishing workflow.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to operate our content pipeline:

  • TikTok OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens, used solely to publish content to our own TikTok account
  • Basic TikTok account metadata returned during the OAuth flow (account ID, display name)
  • Publicly available video performance metrics (views, likes, shares) from our own account

We do not collect, store, or process personal data from TikTok users who view or interact with our content.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through the TikTok API is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate and publish video content to our FounderClips TikTok account
  • Retrieve performance analytics for our own published content
  • Manage our content schedule and posting workflow

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally in our content management environment and are not transmitted to or stored on any third-party servers. We use industry-standard practices to protect stored credentials.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained only as long as they are needed for active content publishing. Tokens are revoked and deleted when access is no longer required.

6. TikTok API Compliance

Our use of the TikTok API complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and API usage policies. We access only the API scopes necessary for content publishing and analytics retrieval on our own account.

7. Your Rights

If you have questions about data held in connection with this service, or wish to request deletion of any information, please contact us at the address below.

8. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact us via our TikTok profile @FounderClips.
